Our Story

Where style meets precision

Doctor Tintz started with a simple idea: window tint should look like it came from the factory, and the experience of getting it done should be just as smooth. From our shop on Dekalb Avenue in Atlanta, we install professional-grade ceramic and carbon films, full color-change wraps, and paint protection film on everything from daily commuters to exotics.

Every job is backed by a lifetime warranty on our tint films because we stand behind the work, not just the product.

  • Lifetime-backed installs

    Premium films, computer-cut patterns, and warranties we honor.

  • Respect for your time

    Clear quotes, honest timelines, and a comfortable place to wait.

  • Atlanta born and based

    Proudly serving drivers across metro Atlanta and beyond.
