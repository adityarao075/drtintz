Appointments and deposits

A deposit is required to reserve an appointment and cover film ordered or cut for your vehicle. Deposits are applied to your final invoice. Please contact us as early as possible if you need to reschedule.

Pricing

Prices listed on this website are starting prices for common vehicles and may vary based on vehicle size, glass configuration, film selection, and existing tint removal. A final quote is confirmed before work begins.

Warranty

Automotive, residential, and commercial tint films are covered by a manufacturer-backed lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, fading, and discoloration. Vinyl wraps are warranted for five years and paint protection film for ten years. Warranties cover the film and installation and exclude damage from accidents, abrasion, improper cleaning, or third-party modifications.

Aftercare

Freshly tinted windows should remain rolled up for three to five days while the film cures. Slight haze or small water pockets during this time are normal and will clear. Issues that remain after the curing period should be reported to us promptly.

Vehicle care

We take reasonable care of every vehicle in our possession. Please remove valuables before drop-off. Pre-existing damage, aftermarket electronics, and defective window mechanisms should be disclosed at check-in.

Legal compliance

Customers are responsible for choosing tint shades that comply with the laws of the state where the vehicle is registered. We are happy to advise on Georgia requirements.
