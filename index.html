FAQ

Everything you need to know

Do you offer a warranty?
Yes. Our ceramic, nano-ceramic, residential, and commercial films are covered by a lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, fading, and discoloration. Vinyl wraps carry a 5-year warranty and paint protection film carries a 10-year warranty. Your coverage is registered at the time of install.
How long will the job take?
Most full-vehicle tint installs take about two to three hours. Larger SUVs, windshields, and tint removals can add time. Wraps and PPF are multi-day projects and are scheduled individually. After tinting, keep your windows rolled up for three to five days while the film fully cures.
Do I wait there or do I drop off my car?
Either works. For standard tint jobs you’re welcome to wait in the shop while we work. For wraps, PPF, or full-day services we recommend dropping off in the morning, and we’ll let you know as soon as your vehicle is ready.
What is the deposit for?
The deposit secures your appointment slot and lets us order and pre-cut the film for your exact vehicle before you arrive. It’s applied in full toward your final invoice. If you need to reschedule, reach out as early as possible and we’ll move your appointment with you.
What other services do you offer?
Beyond automotive window tint, we offer full color-change vinyl wraps, paint protection film, and flat-glass tinting for homes and commercial buildings. Have something else in mind? Send us a message and we’ll let you know how we can help.
