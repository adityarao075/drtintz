Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Dr. Tintz LLC (“Doctor Tintz,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit doctortintz.com or book a service with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide through our contact form or booking system, such as your name, email address, phone number, and vehicle information.
  • Appointment and payment details processed through our third-party scheduling and payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data such as pages visited, device type, and approximate location, collected through standard analytics tools and cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To schedule, confirm, and follow up on appointments.
  • To register and honor product warranties.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To send occasional updates or promotions, which you can opt out of at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our scheduling platform, payment processor, and email provider, and only as needed to deliver our services or comply with the law.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing doctortintz@gmail.com. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link in any email we send.

Contact
