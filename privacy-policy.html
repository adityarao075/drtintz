Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 25, 2026

Tanked Tinterz LLC, doing business as Doctor Tintz ("we," "us," or "our"), is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our services, visit our website, or communicate with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle information (year, make, model)
  • Appointment and scheduling details
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communication preferences

How We Collect Your Information

We collect information when you:

  • Submit a booking or inquiry form on our website
  • Schedule an appointment by phone or in person
  • Provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages
  • Communicate with us via phone, text, or email
  • Make a payment for our services

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Send appointment reminders and updates via SMS
  • Provide customer service and respond to inquiries
  • Send promotional offers and updates (with your consent)
  • Process payments
  • Improve our services

SMS/Text Messaging

By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive text messages from Doctor Tintz. These messages may include:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Booking updates and follow-ups
  • Promotional offers and special deals
  • Customer service communications

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (404) 375-3130.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to assist with our operations, including messaging platforms, payment processors, and scheduling tools. These providers are obligated to protect your information and only use it to perform services on our behalf.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of promotional communications at any time

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
